If a stranger lands on your Facebook page or website on their phone, can they find these four things without scrolling far?

- What you do, in plain words
- Where you are
- When you are open
- How to call you (a number they can tap)

Those are the questions people ask first. Photos, history and specials are good to have, but they come after.

Try it: hand your phone to someone who does not know your business and ask them to find your hours. If it takes them a while, that is the thing to fix first.
