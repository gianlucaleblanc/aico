Thanksgiving is next Thursday. A five-minute job for any business owner this week:

Look at every place your opening hours appear (your Facebook page, your Google listing, your own website if you have one, the sign on the door) and make sure they all say the same thing for Thanksgiving Day and the days around it.

If you are closed, say closed. If you are open short hours, say which hours. A customer who drives over to a locked door because one listing said "open" does not always come back.

No tools needed, just your phone and five minutes.
