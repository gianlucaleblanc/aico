A quick check you can do from your phone today: search for your own business name and town, and open the first few results.

Look at three things in each one:
1. Is the phone number the one you actually answer?
2. Is the address right, including the street number?
3. Is the name spelled the way it is on your sign?

Old numbers and addresses linger in directories for years after a move or a new phone line. Most listings have a "suggest an edit" or "claim this business" link, and fixing it costs nothing but a few minutes.

Write down the one phone number you want customers to use, and make every listing match it.
