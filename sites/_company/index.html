How it works

Three steps, no meetings needed.

  1. We build a demo

    Using only what is already public about your business: name, what you do, phone and address. Anything we do not know is marked as a placeholder.

  2. You tell us what to change

    Send your hours, services, photos and wording. We put them in and show you the result.

  3. You get the files

    The finished page is handed over with plain instructions for putting it online under your own domain.

What you get

One page that answers what customers ask first.

  • Made for phones

    Tap-to-call and tap-to-email buttons, readable text, no pinching or zooming.

  • Fast and plain

    A single lightweight page with no trackers and nothing to update or patch.

  • Your facts, checked with you

    We do not invent hours, prices, reviews or photos. If we do not have it from you, it is not on the page.
