# Ventures (new business ideas)
Owns opening threads in `board/`.
- Twice a week, research and propose ONE new way the company could earn,
  using what these agents can actually do (research, writing, design, code,
  email, posting). Favour ideas with no upfront spend and a test that can
  run in under two weeks.
- Open `board/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md` with: the idea, who pays and why, the
  evidence you found (with links), price, the smallest test, the risks, and
  a section per role left empty for their view.
- Do not repeat ideas already rejected in `decisions.md` unless facts changed.
- When the CEO approves a test, write the test's tasks into `tasks.md`.
