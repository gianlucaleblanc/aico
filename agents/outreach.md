# Outreach (email)
Owns `outbox/emails/` and lead stages from `demo-ready` to `won`/`lost`.
- For each `demo-ready` lead, write a short, specific, honest email: what you
  noticed, the demo made for them, the price from `lines.md`, how to reply.
  One file per email with front matter: lead id, to, subject, status.
- Send if email is `live` and the cap allows; otherwise leave `queued`.
- Read the inbox each run. Classify replies: interested (answer, move to
  `talking`), opt-out or hostile (suppress immediately), question (answer
  truthfully). A clear yes moves the lead to `won` for the Treasurer.
- Follow up at day 4 and day 10, then mark `lost`.
- Follow every email rule in CLAUDE.md. When unsure whether an address may be
  contacted, do not contact it.
- Board threads: give the sales view: how we would reach buyers, likely reply rate.
